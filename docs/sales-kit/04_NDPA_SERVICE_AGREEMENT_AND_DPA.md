## Part A: Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) Agreement Outline

### 1. Parties & Capacity
* **Vendor:** [Crown Tech / Legal Entity TBD upon incorporation] ("Service Provider")
* **Customer:** The Subscribing Educational Institution ("The School")

### 2. Service Scope & Availability
* Access to the Eductive School Management Platform modules selected under the School's subscription.
* Target operational availability during academic term hours, excluding scheduled maintenance.

### 3. Institutional Data Ownership
* The School retains exclusive ownership of all student records, academic scores, and financial data. The Service Provider acts solely as a technological custodian.

---

## Part B: Data Processing Addendum (NDPA 2023 Alignment Outline)

### 1. Statutory Roles
* **Data Controller:** The School determines the lawful basis and purpose of student and staff record processing.
* **Data Processor:** Service Provider processes data strictly on documented instruction from the School.

### 2. Protection of Minors (NDPA Section 31)
* Student data must be processed strictly for legitimate academic, parental communication, and student safety purposes.
* Prohibition on sale, commercial profiling, or advertising usage of student records.

### 3. Security Measures
* Transport encryption (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) and database storage encryption.
* Row-Level Security (RLS) data isolation between institutions.
* Strict role-based permissions with temporary, one-time staff onboarding credentials.

### 4. Cross-Border Data Hosting & Sub-Processors
* Cloud database hosting location and cross-border data transfer compliance in accordance with NDPA Section 41–43 guidelines.
* Formal sub-processor registry to be approved prior to commercial launch.

### 5. Breach Notification (NDPA Section 40)
* Written notification to the School's designated administrator within 72 hours of any confirmed personal data incident.
